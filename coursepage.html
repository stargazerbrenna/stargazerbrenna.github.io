I really enjoyed my study abroad course (GLS 38120). This course had me and 11 other students travel to Munich, Germany for a week during spring break. I found immersing myself in the Language greatly improved my German. I have also never had more fun on a trip! The whole group formed a bond together and I will treasure the memories I made forever. The assignment for this trip was to pick a general theme and find 3 locations pertaining to that theme, and then create a presentation about them. German students had to do the assignment in German. I picked the theme of tea shops!

In AST 1010, I was able to see how skills with computers and programming are applied in real world astronomy. many assignments utilized python and Jupyter Notebook to analyze data, but my favorite assignment used an image editor to compile different layers of colors (red, green, blue) over a monochrome image to create one with color. The end producted was a beautiful nebula! This assignment helped me better understand astronomical photography.
